Call invitation — what each person receives

Rendered with the real email template from gmail-reply and the real markup and styles from index.html, using sample names and links. This is a preview, not a live call.
The journey: agent starts or schedules a call → customer gets the email → clicks Join call → enters a name on the guest screen → connects. Agents on the team get the bell notification instead.

1 · Customer email — call started now

Sent when an agent clicks Video Call on a ticket.

2 · Customer email — scheduled call

Includes the time (with the agent's timezone) and an Add to Google Calendar link.

3 · Guest screen — after the customer clicks Join call

Opens at goobox.necxa.uk/meet/<code>. No account; the browser then asks for camera and mic.

4 · Agent — bell notification and toast

Internal team meetings. Clicking the notification joins the room.

5 · Verified Necxa user — in the app chat

For tickets created in the Necxa app, the same message is also relayed into their chat as plain text. It's a link, not a button — in-app messages have no button yet.

For comparison · a normal reply email

Unchanged: still gets the Buy Now and Track cards. Only call invitations swap them for the Join card.
